# Problem Statement: Reducing Cart Abandonment on MyCoke360

## Business Problem
Swire Coca-Cola is losing money every order period due to cart abandonment, a phenomenon where customers fill their carts with product, but don't place the order by the scheduled date. This problem interferes with plant logistics, revenue, and customer relations. 

## Benefit of a Solution
The purpose of the proposed solution is to prevent cart abandonment, or at the very least, understand why it happens. This project will provide insights into what events or sequence of events correlate with cart abandonment, what causes a customer to return to a previously abandoned cart, what happens after a cart is abandoned, what products appear most frequently in abandoned carts, and what the overall financial impacts of cart abandonment are to Swire Coca-Cola. These insights will benefit Swire Coca-Cola by enabling them to quanitfy (by product and revenue), predict, and prevent cart abandonment. 

## Success Metrics
The current effects of cart abandonment to both plant operations and revenue are unknown. Therefore, our success metrics will investigated and cemented during the Exploratory Data Analysis ("EDA") phase. Once we are able to understand the data provided, and establish a baseline as to the frequency of cart abandonment, then we will be able to establish formal targets. As for now, we will base our benchmark of success off of the frequency of cart abandonment, since all other targets/metrics will stem from the frequency of occurance and its reduction. 

## Analytics Approach
Frequency of cart abandonment will be established using simple statistical methods. The chance a cart will be abandoned will be provided via a machine learning classification model, since we want to give Swire Coca-Cola actionable insights. The classification model will be accompanied by a model confidence for transparency. All other insights will be evalutated using machine learning regression models, as the predictions will be percentages or quantities. 

## Scope
The project will deliver an EDA, a notebook of implementable models, and a presentation on the project's insights. The EDA will serve as the project's quantifiable source of baselines. The notebook of implementable models will serve two purposes. First, as a template for Swire Coca-cola's data science department to implement any models they find useful. Second, as a proof to defend accuracy of the insights. The presentation will serve to communicate the insights discovered, success metrics, and recommendations.
