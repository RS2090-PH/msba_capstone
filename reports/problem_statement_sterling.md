# Problem Statement: Reducing Cart Abandonment on MyCoke360


## Business Problem

Swire recently launched a new website, MyCoke360, which is a B2B platform where businesses can purchase CocaCola products. However, many customers will add items to their online shopping cart without completing a purchase before their order date, otherwise known as ‘cart abandonment’. This is problematic in a variety of ways. Not only does cart abandonment signal a loss of a sale, but it can also signal wasted market spending and potentially lead to inventory management issues. Furthermore, it could be indicative of underlying problems in brand perception, user-friendliness, or potential pricing, which could give competitors an opportunity in the market.


## Benefit of a Solution

By reducing cart abandonment, Swire can not only increase revenue through immediate customer purchases, but they can improve other areas of the business as well. Reduced cart abandonment can eliminate skew in data analytics so they can better understand which products or promotions are most effective. They can also set a baseline for customer orders, so they can better manage inventory, and identify other areas of improvement previously hidden by the problem.  They can also enhance the MyCoke360 platform to improve overall customer experience. Without cart abandonment, Swire would be better equipped to understand their customer’s needs and trends in the market, with more room to maneuver in pricing, sales, and marketing.  



## Analytics Approach

This will be both a descriptive and predictive analytics project set to take place over the course of 3 months. We will begin with an exploratory data analysis taking information from Swire’s Google Analytics platform, and other internal sources. The data will include webpage interactions, order information, customer information, product information, shipping information, and sales information. 

After the data has been cleaned and normalized, we will identify the best definition for cart abandonment, taking into account customer patterns and business needs. We will then identify useful predictors, highly-correlated variables, and other data trends that could help predict cart abandonment. 

Near the end of the project we will build a model to see how accurately we can predict cart abandonment. We can then take the top predictors and use them to identify ways to reduce cart abandonment. 



## Success Metrics

We will measure the success of the project in terms of reduction of cart abandonment. Moreover, we hope to improve the conversion rate between an item being placed in a customer’s cart and then purchased within the given order window, without dropping the number of customers or the items they place in their carts. 

Other areas of success would include the identification of predictive variables in customer behavior, which can then be used by Swire for marketing, promotions, or other process improvements.
